01 — Framing

You are not building a map. You are building a dataset with a map on it.

bengaluru.rent works because of information asymmetry: a broker quotes ₹38k for a flat whose sitting tenant pays ₹26k, and nobody can check. The map is just the cheapest way to make thousands of small private truths legible at once. Harshit Anand built it solo in April 2026 and word-of-mouth carried it to ~7,400 pins.

The builder's own framing confirms this. He posted it on LinkedIn as "vibe-coded www.bengaluru.rent" — the code was the cheap part, written with AI assistance in evenings. At that post the map held ₹150 Cr of pinned rent; it now holds ₹333 Cr. The engineering didn't more than double in that window. The dataset did.

So the engineering is the easy half. A MapLibre canvas with clustered pins and a submit sheet is two focused weekends. The half that kills clones is the first thousand data points — because a rent map with 40 pins doesn't look sparse, it looks broken, and nobody shares a broken site.

The asset

Paid rents

Self-reported by tenants, anonymous, dated, tied to a specific society. Nobody else has this.

The hook

Zero brokerage

Owner-direct listings. Gurugram's broker norm is one month's rent + GST — that's a ₹60k–1L saving on a decent flat.

The moat

Time

Every month of pins compounds — ₹150 Cr to ₹333 Cr pinned in a matter of months. A competitor starting a year later starts at zero again.

02 — Local physics

Copying Bengaluru's product one-for-one would build the wrong thing

Bengaluru rents are discussed by neighbourhood — "Indiranagar 2BHK". Gurugram rents are discussed by building — "Ireo Victory Valley 3BHK", "Vatika City", "DLF Phase 3 builder floor". The city is condo-dominated and vertically stacked, which changes almost every design decision downstream.

Primary key

Society, not neighbourhood

Pin to a canonical society from a typeahead list, then let the sector polygon be derived. A society with 900 units and 30 pins gives a real median; a 200 m radius of scattered points does not.

Free geography

The sector grid is your basemap

Sectors 1–115, the DLF phases, Sushant Lok, South City, Palam Vihar, old-Gurgaon colonies. Clean, well-known, mutually exclusive polygons — an aggregation layer handed to you for nothing.

The lie in "rent"

All-in monthly, or it's useless

CAM/maintenance runs roughly ₹3–8 per sq ft per month and is often quoted separately. Power backup is billed per unit on top. A ₹45k "rent" can be ₹58k out the door. Capture the components; display the total.

Stock types

Three different products

Society tower, builder floor, independent house/kothi — plus PG/co-living. They price differently for the same BHK on the same road. One field, four values, never mixed in a median.

The real filter

Commute, not distance

Nobody searches "within 5 km". They search "under 30 min to Cyber City / Udyog Vihar / Golf Course Road". Drive-time bands beat radius search here more than in any other Indian city.

Also priced in

The NCR checklist

Deposit months (2–3 here, not Bengaluru's 6–10), water source, power-backup hours, non-veg and pet policy, bachelor-allowed, society tenant rules. These decide a shortlist and cost nothing to collect.

Name the site correctly

Residents overwhelmingly say Gurgaon; the state renamed it Gurugram in 2016. Register both gurgaon.rent and gurugram.rent and redirect one to the other. Search volume and word-of-mouth favour "Gurgaon" — I'd make that the primary and keep Gurugram as the formal alias.

03 — Data model

Get these six fields right and the rest is decoration

Two fields do disproportionate work. source separates what a tenant pays from what a broker asks — that distinction is the product, so it must be structural, not a note. lease_start_month makes a rent meaningful: after the 20–35% surge across the Cyber City and Golf Course Extension corridors, a 2023 rent and a 2026 rent are different facts.

-- Postgres + PostGIS. One table does the whole MVP.

create table pins (
  id              uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  created_at      timestamptz default now(),

  -- geography: store the SNAPPED point only, never the raw one
  geom            geometry(Point, 4326),   -- rounded to ~100 m
  society_id      uuid references societies(id),
  sector_code     text,                    -- 'SEC-57', 'DLF-3', 'SUSHANT-LOK-1'

  -- the unit
  bhk             text,   -- 1RK | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5+
  stock_type      text,   -- society_tower | builder_floor | independent | pg
  carpet_sqft     int,
  floor_no        int,
  furnishing      text,   -- bare | semi | full

  -- the money, decomposed
  rent_inr           int not null,
  maintenance_inr    int,     -- monthly CAM, 0 if included
  maintenance_basis  text,    -- included | fixed | per_sqft
  power_backup_note  text,
  deposit_months     numeric,

  -- ⚑ the two fields that make this trustworthy
  source             text not null,  -- tenant_paid | owner_ask | broker_ask | tolet_board
  lease_start_month  date not null,  -- when this rent was actually agreed

  -- moderation
  status          text default 'pending',  -- pending | live | rejected
  submitter_hash  text,                    -- salted hash of IP; never the IP
  flags           jsonb
);

Build a societies table before anything else. Seed it from OpenStreetMap — named landuse=residential and building=apartments polygons in Gurugram cover most large condos, and OSM's ODbL licence permits this with attribution. Hand-clean the top 300 by name and add sector, developer, and approximate unit count. This single table is what turns scattered pins into "Ireo Uptown: 2BHK median ₹52k, n=14, freshest Jun 2026".

04 — Stack

One non-obvious call: don't use Google Maps

bengaluru.rent renders on Google Maps, and at 172k visitors that is a live billing risk — Google's Dynamic Maps free allowance is on the order of 10k map loads a month, and it's roughly $7 per 1,000 loads after that (check current SKU pricing before you commit). MapLibre GL JS with Protomaps tiles you host yourself on Cloudflare R2 is visually equivalent, costs effectively nothing, and can't surprise you with a four-figure invoice in month three.

05 — Cold start

Seed 500 pins privately, then launch once

You get one good launch post. Spend it on a map that already looks alive. Target roughly 30–40 pins in each of ~15 micro-markets before the site is public — Gurugram's condo density means fewer pins go further than in Bengaluru, because 30 pins in one society is a genuinely useful median.

The fifteen micro-markets to cover

DLF Phases 1–5 · Sushant Lok 1–3 · South City 1–2 · Golf Course Road (42/43/53/54) · Golf Course Ext Rd (58–68) · Sohna Road (47–49) · Sector 56/57 · Nirvana Country & Sector 50 · New Gurgaon (80–95) · Dwarka Expressway (99–113) · Palam Vihar · Old Gurgaon (4–7, 14, 15) · Sectors 21–23 near Udyog Vihar · MG Road / Sikanderpur · Sectors 70–76

Highest yield

Society WhatsApp groups

Gurugram's RWA and tower groups are unusually active and unusually large. One admin who likes the idea is worth 50 pins. Go society by society.

Highest yield

LinkedIn, not just Reddit

Gurugram's renter base is corporate — consulting, tech, BFSI in Cyber City and Udyog Vihar. A LinkedIn post reaches this city's tenants better than it would in Bengaluru. Then r/gurgaon, r/delhi, and Blind.

Weekend work

Walk the To-Let boards

Photograph boards in DLF Phase 3, Sector 45, Sushant Lok, Palam Vihar. Ship this as a user feature too — "Spot a To-Let" — but seed it yourself first.

Owner-direct

Facebook & OLX owner posts

"Gurgaon Flats & Flatmates — No Brokerage" style groups and OLX owner listings. Tag every one of these owner_ask, never tenant_paid.

Do not scrape the portals

NoBroker, MagicBricks, Housing and 99acres all prohibit scraping in their terms, and their data is asking prices — the exact number your product exists to contradict. Seeding with scraped asks would make your medians wrong and hand a competitor a cease-and-desist. Public listings are fine as leads to verify by hand; they are not a database to copy.

06 — Sequence

Four to six weeks, part time

Phase 0Weekend 1

Societies table + domains

Pull Gurugram residential polygons from OSM, clean the top 300 by hand, attach sector codes. Register both domains. This is unglamorous and it unblocks everything else.

Phase 1Weekends 2–3

Map + submit flow

MapLibre canvas, sector-level counts at low zoom, society pins at high zoom, a bottom sheet with median by BHK. Submit must be under 60 seconds: society typeahead, BHK, rent, maintenance, furnishing, month. No account, ever.

Phase 2Weeks 3–4

Seed to 500, in private

Site is live but unannounced. Work the WhatsApp groups and walk the boards. Watch which micro-markets stay empty and go get those specifically — a hole in DLF Phase 4 is more damaging than 50 extra pins in Sector 57.

Phase 3Week 5

Launch

One coordinated day: r/gurgaon, LinkedIn, Blind, the society groups that helped seed it. Lead with a finding, not a feature — "here's what 500 Gurgaon tenants actually pay, by society" travels; "I built a map" doesn't.

Phase 4Post-launch

Owner listings, then everything else

Zero-brokerage owner listings are the revenue-adjacent hook and the reason people return. Flatmates, drive-time bands, green cover, and metro layers come after — each is a week, none is urgent.

07 — Integrity

Brokers will try to poison it. Plan for that on day one.

Anonymous submission is what makes people honest, and it's also the attack surface. In Gurugram the motivated attacker isn't a troll — it's an agent with a stake in a society's headline rent staying high. Four defences, all cheap:

  • Statistical flagging. Anything beyond ~2.5σ of the existing society+BHK distribution goes to the queue instead of the map. New societies with fewer than three pins are always queued.
  • Rate limits per salted IP hash plus Turnstile. A single actor filing 20 pins into one society in an hour is the signal you're looking for.
  • Show n and freshness everywhere. "₹52k median · 14 reports · latest Jun 2026" is honest and makes thin data self-evidently thin. Medians, never means — one fake ₹5L pin can't move a median.
  • Let readers report a pin. Two reports on a pin with n≥5 pulls it for review. Cheap crowd immune system.
08 — Privacy & law

Anonymous by construction, not by promise

  • Snap coordinates before storage. Three decimal places is about 110 m. Round on the server on insert and discard the original — if you never hold the precise point, you can't leak it.
  • Suppress small-n societies. In a 12-unit builder floor, one pin plus a floor number identifies a specific tenant's rent to their landlord. Don't surface society-level figures below three pins; show the sector instead.
  • Never store tower or flat numbers. Not in a free-text note either — strip digits from notes matching unit patterns.
  • DPDP Act 2023. Anonymous tenant pins with no contact details sit largely outside its scope. Owner listings carrying a phone number do not: you need a clear consent notice at collection, a stated purpose, a working deletion route, and a named grievance contact on the site.
  • Ads and data. If you run ads to cover servers, say so plainly on the About page — and then never sell the pin data, because that promise is the entire reason people submit.

10 — Honest risks

Where this most likely dies

Most likely

Cold start

You launch at 120 pins because seeding was boring, the map looks empty, nobody shares it, and it never compounds. This is the failure mode. Guard against it by refusing to launch under 500.

Likely

Second-city brand transfer

bengaluru.rent's trust doesn't come with you. Gurugram doesn't know you, and NCR is more broker-saturated and more sceptical. Budget more effort for credibility than the Bengaluru timeline suggests.

Possible

Poisoned medians

If agents successfully inflate a few large societies and someone notices publicly, the trust is gone and doesn't come back. Ship the flagging in v1, not v2.

Watch

Incumbent response

NoBroker and Housing operate heavily in NCR. They won't copy the crowdsourced-rent angle quickly, but they can outspend you on SEO for every generic query. Own society and sector long-tail pages instead.

11 — Start here

The next three things, in order

  • Register the domains today — gurgaon.rent and gurugram.rent. Cheapest irreversible step; do it before you write code.
  • Build the societies table from OSM and hand-clean the top 300. Nothing else works properly without it, and it's the one asset a competitor can't clone in an afternoon.
  • Get 50 pins from people you know, on a Google Form if you must, before building any UI. If 50 is hard, 500 is impossible and you've learned that for the price of a weekend rather than a month.

Ship the map second. It's the part you can definitely finish.
